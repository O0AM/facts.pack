# Authors

## Inventor & author

**Aditya Mishra** — <adityamishra1477@gmail.com>

Sole inventor and author of the FactsPack (`.pack`) format: the
line-prefixed tab-separated grammar, the dictionary-interning scheme, the
incremental `+`/`x` row operations, the master/diff hash-chain design and
its prompt-cache pinning model, the in-band legend / integrity-trailer /
untrusted-data / freshness trust laws, and the two profiles (the tabular
map-pack and the prose audit fix-pack).

The format was created for the FACTs (Fun AI Coding Tools) project family —
**factstack** (codebase mapping for AI agents) and **facts+** (UI,
accessibility, and trust auditing) — and is open-sourced here under the
GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).

- Format v0.1: design-locked 2026-04-24
- Format v0.2 (current standard): specified and implemented June 2026
