# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and AI-agent builders evaluating or adopting the FactsPack
(`.pack`) format. They arrive from GitHub, search, or a colleague's link,
usually skeptical: "another format?" Secondary audience: AI coding agents
reading the docs programmatically (llms.txt, FACTSPACK_PROMPT.md). Context:
desk, large screen, reading mode; they skim first, then read deeply if the
first screen earns trust. The job to be done: understand what `.pack` is in
five minutes, verify the claims are real, decide whether to adopt.

## Product Purpose

These pages are the public face of an open-source data format whose entire
pitch is rigor: self-describing artifacts, integrity trailers, honest
benchmarks, "cite or refuse." The site succeeds when a cold reader can read
a real pack unaided after one visit, and when none of the claims feel
inflated. Success metric: adoption and citations, driven by credibility.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, honest, quietly confident. A specification with warmth: precise
language, real bytes shown as first-class exhibits, caveats stated in the
open rather than buried. Three words: exacting, candid, crafted.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: purple gradients, hero-metric template,
  identical icon-card grids, glassmorphism.
- Documentation-first pages that lecture before delivering value; the site
  must feel like a quick, fun mobile app: tool first, theory after
  (owner decision, 2026-06-12).
- Crypto-landing neon, AI-startup gradient text, stock 3D illustrations.
- Marketing pages that hide limitations; this brand states them.

Owner-locked aesthetic (2026-06-12): the Tokyo-Night-style dark scheme and
gradient bar charts from `docs/v0.index.html` are the chosen visual identity
for the app page; the editorial paper look is archived at
`docs/v1.editorial.index.html`.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what the format preaches.** The pages must be as
   self-describing, precise, and honest as the format itself.
2. **The document is the interface.** Editorial, specification-grade
   typography and structure (numbered sections, figures, exhibits,
   colophon), not app chrome.
3. **Show real bytes.** Actual pack excerpts are the hero artifacts,
   presented like exhibits with captions, never decorative screenshots.
4. **Honesty over hype.** Benchmark caveats and collision notes stay
   visible at the same hierarchy as the claims they qualify.
5. **One family, two voices.** The main explainer is the formal voice; the
   caveman ELI5 is the playful sibling. Shared bones (structure, quality
   bar), different registers; the joke page still meets the craft bar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum. Visible keyboard focus on all interactive
elements. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (decorative animation off).
Semantic HTML landmarks and heading order. Body text at readable measure
(65 to 75 characters). Light-first design with a true dark variant, both
meeting contrast.
